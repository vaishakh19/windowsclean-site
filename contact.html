Technical support

Report bugs and ask product questions in the Windows Clean issue tracker. It is public, so do not include license keys, personal files, or private information.

Privacy, billing, and legal requests

The public issue tracker is for technical support only. Do not post license keys there. Live checkout remains disabled until the owner publishes a private billing/privacy contact and final purchase terms. Test transactions are not live sales.
