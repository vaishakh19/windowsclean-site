Privacy summary

Your scan stays on your PC.

Windows Clean scans files on your Windows device and keeps scan history, preferences, and license state in local app storage. The app does not send scan contents to Dodo Payments.

If you activate a Pro license in a configured build, the app sends the license key and a device label to Dodo Payments to activate or validate that license. Checkout session creation and payment verification run on the Windows Clean Vercel backend.
