Privacy summary

Your scan stays on your PC.

Windows Clean scans files on your Windows device and keeps scan history, preferences, and license state in local app storage. The app does not send scan contents to Dodo Payments.

If you activate a Pro license in a configured build, Windows Clean sends the license key and a device label to Dodo Payments to activate or validate that license. The current website has no checkout form and does not sell Pro.

For technical help, use the Windows Clean issue tracker. Please don’t post license keys or private information in a public issue. A private privacy contact and complete paid-product terms must be added before checkout is enabled.
